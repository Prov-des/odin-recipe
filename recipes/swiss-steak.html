Swiss Steak Recipe

The Great Dish

Swiss sticks are a delicious fried cheese snack made from Gruyère cheese, popular in western
Switzerland and often referred to as Malakoff de Vinzel.

These savory treats are part of Swiss culinary tradition, especially in the Vaud region. If you're ever in western Switzerland, especially in Vaud, trying Malakoff-style Swiss sticks is a must for cheese lovers.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup of all-purpose flower
  • ½ teaspoon of salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 (2 pounds) beef round steak, 1 inch thick
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable shortening
  • ¼ cup of water, or as needed
  • 1 (8 pounds) diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, minced
  • ½ green bell pepper, chopped
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps and Direction

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Mix flour, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper together in a bowl
  3. Sprinkle half the flour mixture on one side of round steak pieces; pound steak until coating is absorbed. Flip steak and coat with remaining flour mixture; pound steak until coating is absorbed
  4. Cut steak into 6 pieces.
  5. Melt shortening in a large skillet over medium heat; place steak pieces in the hot shortening. Cook until browned, 7 to 10 minutes per side. Cover and simmer until tender, about 1 hour, adding water as needed
  6. Turn steak pieces over and add tomatoes, onion, and green bell pepper to the skillet. Season with salt and black pepper
  7. Simmer until vegetables are tender, about 30 more minutes
  8. Your awesome dish is ready and ready to be served
